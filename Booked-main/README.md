HOW TO OPEN WEBSITE

1. Open xaamp turn it on (Mysql, Apache)
2. open http://localhost/phpmyadmin
3. Click on the "Databases" tab.
4. Under "Create database," type the name books
5. Leave the "Collation" as is, and click "Create"
6. Click on the books database you just created, Click on the "SQL" tab at the top.
7. Open your dbqueries file, copy all the SQL commands
8. Paste all those commands into the big text box in the "SQL" tab
9. Click the "Go" button
10. Go to your XAMPP installation folder (usually C:\xampp).
11. Open the htdocs folder.
12. Copy Booked-main folder to htdocs
13. http://localhost/Booked-main/login.php or register.php
